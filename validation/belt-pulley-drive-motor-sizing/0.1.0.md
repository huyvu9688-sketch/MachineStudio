## Purpose and Supported Applications

Given a belt-and-pulley linear drive's own pulley geometry and mass, total
moving mass (a rigid carriage/table attached to the belt), belt mass,
friction, orientation, gear ratio, and one commanded accelerate-to-speed
motion event, computes the required motor specifications: load torque,
acceleration torque, maximum momentary torque, required torque with an
engineer-supplied safety factor, operating speed, required power, total
reflected system inertia, and inertia ratio. Reports required-spec values
and pass/fail checks against engineer-supplied margins, the same scope
every prior Motor Sizing Tool module already established. Does not check
belt tension, belt width/pitch, tooth shear, or wrap angle, select a
gearbox/coupling/drive/brake, or match a motor catalog (ADR-0011 "Output
scope").

## Validity Envelope and Assumptions

- One belt-and-pulley linear drive: one motor-driven pulley plus one idler
  pulley of equal pitch diameter (every source's own worked example
  assumes this), one rigid carriage/table rigidly attached to the belt
  (not a conveyor's loose load — `direct-drive-conveyor-motor-sizing@
  0.1.0`'s own "Relationship to Existing and Planned Modules" table already
  draws this line), direct-connected or through a single fixed gear ratio.
- One accelerate-to-speed motion event — not a repeating duty cycle, no
  return move.
- Horizontal, vertical, or inclined orientation
  (`0 <= incline_angle <= 90 deg`) — both primary sources carry an
  explicit gravity term. Not independently validated at an intermediate
  incline from a published worked example (the one located example is
  horizontal).
- Self-contained per ADR-0011 "Reuse policy": reproduces, rather than
  imports, Oriental Motor Co., Ltd.'s own combined wire-belt/rack-and-pinion
  sizing method. The one genuine import is `lib/engine/mechanics` (Unit
  6.1).
- No belt tension, belt width/pitch, tooth-shear, or wrap-angle selection,
  and no motor catalog matching.

## Reference Examples (Published Worked Examples)

**AutomationDirect's own "Belt Drive - Example Calculations" (pp.
B-11-B-13)**, reproduced through the real `executeModule` compute path
(`automationdirect-reference-example.test.ts`): a 90 lb table+workpiece,
`mu=0.05`, horizontal, 10:1-geared, 2.0 in-diameter aluminum pulley pair
(mass derived from its own printed geometry and density), stroke 50 in
over a 4.0 s move with 1.0 s accel/decel.

- `pulley_inertia` (both pulleys, `(1/8)*(M_drive+M_idler)*D^2`) matches
  the source's own printed `J_pulleys=0.0006 lb-in-s^2`
  (`stage-1-spec.md`'s own recomputed `0.000598`) within `0.2%`. This
  figure carries no mechanical-efficiency term in either source's own
  convention, so it is an unqualified reproduction.
- `load_inertia` (carriage-only component) and the reflected-to-motor
  inertia are **not** claimed to equal the source's own printed
  `J_W=0.291 lb-in-s^2` / `J_(pulleys+load) to motor=0.0029 lb-in-s^2`
  figures directly — both bake in AutomationDirect's own disclosed
  convention of dividing the carriage's own inertia by mechanical
  efficiency (`e=0.8`), a convention this module does not follow (see
  "Tolerances and Deviations" below). Reapplying that exact,
  already-disclosed `1/e` factor at the test level (not inside this
  module's own kernel) reproduces both printed figures within `0.1%`,
  proving the underlying carriage-inertia physics is correct.
- `load_torque`, `momentary_torque`, and `required_torque` are **not**
  reproduced — see "Tolerances and Deviations."
- `acceleration_torque` and `inertia_ratio` are exercised end to end (the
  compute path runs without error and produces a positive, correctly
  ordered result) but not asserted against the source's own printed
  `T_accel=0.46 lb-in` (recomputed `0.4649`) or `inertia ratio=9.6` — see
  "Unsupported Conditions."

## Independent Method or Tool Comparison

**Satisfied (2026-08-13).** `independent-benchmark.test.ts` cross-checks
this module's own two-function force/load-torque kernel
(`resolveDriveForce` then `resolveLoadTorque`) against a single combined
expression reimplementing the identical Oriental Motor formula, written
independently in `independent-benchmark.ts` — the same "structurally
separate reimplementation, proved identical" pattern
`direct-drive-conveyor-motor-sizing@0.1.0`'s own Omron benchmark and
`rack-pinion-motor-sizing@0.1.0`'s own Andantex cross-check both establish.

- Independent method/tool: a single-expression reimplementation of
  Oriental Motor's own `F = FA + m*g*(sin(theta)+mu*cos(theta))`,
  `TL = F*D/(2*eta*i)`.
- Case(s) compared: one hand-verified horizontal scenario, plus a
  deterministic 300-scenario property sweep (mass, incline, friction,
  external force, diameter, efficiency, and gear ratio all varied,
  including inclined orientations).
- Result: agreement to floating-point precision (`< 1e-9` relative
  difference) across every scenario — an algebraic identity, not an
  approximation.

## Tolerances and Deviations

- **A real, disclosed disagreement between the two primary sources on
  where mechanical efficiency applies**, verified by hand as genuinely
  different, not a rounding artifact: Oriental Motor divides load torque
  by `eta`; AutomationDirect divides the carriage's own inertia by `e` and
  leaves running torque underated. This module follows Oriental Motor's
  own convention, matching every already-released Motor Sizing Tool
  sibling. Consequently this module's own `load_torque`, `momentary_torque`,
  and `required_torque` do not reproduce AutomationDirect's own printed
  figures, and this module's own `load_inertia`, `reflected_load_inertia`,
  and `inertia_ratio` outputs are smaller than AutomationDirect's own
  printed figures by the disclosed, precisely quantified `1/e=1.25x`
  factor on the carriage-inertia term specifically (see "Reference
  Examples" above) — not an unexplained residual.
- **A confirmed arithmetic slip in AutomationDirect's own worked example**,
  disclosed and not reproduced: its own friction force is computed as
  `0.05 x 100 = 5.0 lb`, though the stated table+workpiece weight is
  `90 lb` (the correct product is `4.5 lb`). This module's own kernel
  computes friction from the actual supplied mass, so it does not
  reproduce the source's own printed `T_run=0.50 lb-in` or
  `T_motor=0.96 lb-in` totals that follow from it — the third such
  source-internal slip this project has found and recorded rather than
  silently matched (after `direct-drive-conveyor-motor-sizing@0.1.0`'s own
  p. F-9 inertia figure and `rack-pinion-motor-sizing@0.1.0`'s own Atlanta
  `Futab` inconsistency).

## Unsupported Conditions

- Unequal drive/idler pulley diameters — no source found gives this
  formula; both pulleys share one `pulley_pitch_diameter`.
- A repeating duty cycle or effective (RMS) torque check.
- Belt tension, belt width/pitch, tooth-shear, or wrap-angle selection.
- Motor catalog matching or part selection.
- `acceleration_torque` and `inertia_ratio` numeric reproduction against
  AutomationDirect's own printed worked-example figures — both depend on a
  candidate motor rotor inertia the source's own worked example does not
  print as an independent figure, and back-solving it two different ways
  from the source's own two printed downstream figures (the `T_accel`
  value and the `9.6` inertia ratio) disagrees by roughly 15-20%, most
  plausibly compounding rounding across the source's own multiple
  low-precision (2-4 significant figure) intermediate results. A genuine,
  disclosed evidence gap found during this module's own Stage 3/4 work,
  not a defect in this module's own kernel.
- No `linear-axis@1` or other guided-workflow role — standalone in `0.1.0`
  (ADR-0011).

## Supported Use Limits (Summary)

One belt-and-pulley linear drive, two equal-diameter pulleys, an optional
translating belt mass (default 0), direct-connected or single-fixed-gear-
ratio, one accelerate-to-speed motion event, horizontal/vertical/inclined
orientation. One required safety factor (`>= 1`) and one required maximum
inertia ratio, no built-in defaults. Validated via AutomationDirect's own
published worked example, partially reproduced with every gap precisely
quantified and disclosed (pulley inertia exact; load/reflected inertia
exact after reapplying the source's own disclosed efficiency convention;
load/momentary/required torque not reproduced due to a source arithmetic
slip plus the efficiency-convention difference; acceleration torque and
inertia ratio not numerically asserted due to an underdetermined candidate
motor rotor inertia), plus a 300-scenario independent-benchmark property
sweep on the force/load-torque formula. Released and registered as
`belt-pulley-drive-motor-sizing@0.1.0` 2026-08-13 — the fourth module in
the Motor Sizing Tool family (ADR-0011).
